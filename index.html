1. ONMETOISM

Onmetoism/Temnoism is the founding metaphysical belief system of "Ktisism".

Onmetoism seeks to explain the true foundation of existence, and not mere baseless inevitability of either everything or nothing, and it seeks to find the only potential way existence is possible.

Ktisism is a metaphysical belief that "Nature" makes existence with the potential of two and three elements, one in the middle, two below and above (...or Ktisism placed into the most and least comprehensible manner).

Ktisism defends metaphysics as the only true philosophical branch, as it defines philosophy based upon one's answering of "Nature" of one "Nature", and not one's questioning of unnature of one unnature.

Onmetoism is a perfectly compatible belief system and philosophy to be combined with a religion which merely worships one or more deities, but Onmetoism can also serve as a primary religion.

"Témnō" in the Onmetoist definition is to draw an edge towards division and union, and it is reversed to "Ōnmét-o" in the name of Onmetoism due to its triple duality of Nature.

It has three forms: Emotion Onmetoism, Instinct Onmetoism, and Divine Onmetoism; the path of emotion, the path of instinct, and the path of potential, or the cycle of division rather than the spiral of union.

A Temnoist is a recent initiate believer in Onmetoism who is still in pursuit of the conflict of consciousness and awareness.

A Temnoist becomes an Onmetoist once he is able to conceive the friction of consciousness and awareness.

The text of Onmetoism is strictly intended to be read personally and not to be translated, as every misinterpreted word is harmful; it is not intended to be interpreted in one's own words, but in one's own thoughts; words are often not enough to describe Onmetoism.

Onmetoism is grammatically undescribable, for there is not a more ironic act other than being forced to define definitions while using foreign words; every single fragment of the text is purposeful.

Those who overshrink and share this text despite these risks are not to be viewed as a representation of Onmetoism, but of Temnoism.
